# Ben Wiebers
Assistant Director of Career Services, MS in Applied Data Science
Ben Wiebers is the Assistant Director of Career Services for the Master’s in Applied Data Science (MS-ADS) program. He brings a holistic approach to career advising, with a special emphasis on online students within the MS-ADS program. Prior to working at the university, Ben was a technical recruiter, helping to bring staff of all levels into niche technical roles across Chicago startups and enterprise companies in the US. Regardless of the setting, Ben centers his work on reflection and lifelong learning to enable others to identify and reach their goals. He holds a bachelor’s degree in Mathematics from the University of Wyoming and a master’s degree in Education from Loyola University Chicago.
