# Jose Alvarado
Senior Associate Director, Enrollment Management, MS in Applied AI
Jose Alvarado serves as the Senior Associate Director of Enrollment Management for the Applied AI (AAI) and Applied Data Science (ADS) programs. Prior to his current role, Jose served as a higher education professional specializing in recruitment and enrollment management. He has worked at great institutions around the Chicagoland area, such as the University of Illinois System, Northwestern University, and Loyola University Chicago. He brings with him extensive knowledge of working with undergraduate, graduate, and international student populations.
