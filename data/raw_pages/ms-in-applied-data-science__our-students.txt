# Our Students
Our students are the best part of our Master's in Applied Data Science program. Meet some of our current students!

## Building AI to Make Everyday Life Easier
After enrolling in the University of Chicago’s online MS in Applied Data Science program, Dharti developed the technical skills and confidence to begin building an AI-powered meal planning app that creates personalized meal plans and grocery lists to simplify everyday decision-making. Along the way, she found something just as valuable: a supportive community that encouraged her to pursue an idea she never thought she could build.
