# William Jones
Academic Affairs Specialist, MS in Applied Data Science
William Jones serves as the Academic Affairs Specialist supporting Other Academic Appointees for the University of Chicago’s MS in Applied Data Science program. In this role, he manages academic personnel processes including appointments, reappointments, and onboarding for instructional staff, coordinates hiring and compliance documentation, and supports program operations by collaborating with faculty and administrative partners to ensure timely processing, clear communication, and a positive experience for academic appointees.
William earned a Bachelor of Science in Accounting from Chicago State University. He brings a diverse background in compliance, operations, and program administration spanning higher education, law enforcement, and financial regulation.
