# Lauren Milewski, MSEd
Instructional Designer, MS in Applied Data Science
Lauren is the Instructional Designer for the MS in Applied Data Science program. She helps instructors craft learning experiences that engage students in rigorous academic work and prepare them for fulfilling careers. Most recently, Lauren was the Senior Director of Learning and Design at Kepler College in Rwanda, where she led the design of their first bachelor’s degrees. Lauren is interested in how programs can promote educational equity and align with workforce needs, with a focus on helping students develop 21st century skills that enable them to be lifelong learners. Lauren holds an MSEd from Hunter College and a BA in English and Anthropology from the University of Michigan.
