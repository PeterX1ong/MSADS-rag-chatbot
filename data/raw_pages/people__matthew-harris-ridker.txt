# Matthew Harris-Ridker
Assistant Director, Enrollment Management, MS in Applied Data Science
Matthew Harris-Ridker serves as the Assistant Director of Enrollment Management for the MS in Applied Data Science program. Prior to his current role, Matthew has served as a higher education professional working in recruitment and enrollment management, and in study abroad recruitment and on-site student support. Matthew received his BA from The College of Wooster.
