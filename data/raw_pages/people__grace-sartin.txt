# Grace Sartin
Assistant Director of Instructional Services, MS in Applied Data Science
Grace Sartin serves as the Assistant Director of Instructional Services for the University of Chicago MS in Applied Data Science program. She manages the hiring and support of Instructional Assistants and assists with program development. Prior to her current role, Grace provided academic support to the Art History doctoral students at the University of Chicago and managed student milestone data collection. She has received a B.A. in Art History from Pomona College and is currently completing her M.Ed in Educational Policy, Organization, and Leadership from University of Illinois Urbana Champaign. Her current research focuses on making higher education more accessible for minority populations and she is passionate about training instructional assistants and professors to better support students on their academic path.
