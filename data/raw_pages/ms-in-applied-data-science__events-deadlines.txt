# Events & Deadlines
Welcome to the Events & Deadlines page for the MS in Applied Data Science program! Here, you'll find all the latest updates, upcoming events, important deadlines, and additional news related to our exciting program. Stay informed and engaged with everything happening in the world of data science.

## Upcoming Events

#### Virtual Information Session – Ask A Student
Are you curious about life as a student in the University of Chicago’s MS in Applied Data Science program? Do you have burning questions about coursework, projects, or the overall student experience? Well, you’re in luck! We’re thrilled to announce our upcoming virtual info session, “Ask a Student,” where you can get all your queries answered by a current student. The MS in Applied Data Science “Ask a Student” info session allows prospective students the opportunity to meet virtually with current students in the MS in Applied Data Science program.
Student Bio: Shane Dunkle is a Language Pedagogy Specialist and Junior Data Analyst at the University of Chicago English Language Institute. After 25 years teaching English in Japan and the U.S., he enrolled in the MS in Applied Data Science while working full time at the University, and now applies the coursework directly in his job, building enrollment forecasting models and dashboards for the ELI while continuing to teach. He has focused on NLP and LLMs, which lets him connect his language background to what he is learning in the program. He expects to finish the program in Winter 2027.

#### Virtual Information Session – Online Program
The MS in Applied Data Science program at UChicago integrates business strategy, project-based learning, simulations, case studies, and specific electives addressing the data needs of various industry sectors. Through partnerships with key employers, the program also provides students with applied projects and data sets as well as access to career networks and employment pathways upon graduation. Join us for an online information session to learn program details, admission requirements, and how you can get started.

## Deadlines
All program offerings, including the part-time online program, begin in Autumn. Curious about the academic calendar? Please use the following link to access current and future calendar years: Academic Calendar
In-Person:
- November 9, 2026 – First Priority Application Deadline
- December 7, 2026 – Second Priority Application Deadline
- January 26, 2027 – International Application Deadline (requiring visa sponsorship from UChicago)
- March 4, 2027 – Third Priority Application Deadline
- May 6, 2027 – Fourth Priority Application Deadline
- June 23, 2027 – Final Application Deadline
In-Person application decisions are released approximately 1 to 2 months after each respected deadline.
Online:
- Autumn 2027
- June 23, 2027 – Final Application Deadline
Online application decisions are released on a rolling basis. The deadline represents the last date applications are accepted for the respected start date.
