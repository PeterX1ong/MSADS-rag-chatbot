# International Students
Build skills in AI, machine learning, and data engineering alongside a global cohort, while preparing for careers in tech, healthcare, consulting, and more!

## Applying as an International Student
International students are an important part of the MS in Applied Data Science community, bringing global perspectives into every classroom.
The program offers different formats:
- Only the full-time, in-person program is eligible for student visa sponsorship
- The online program is open to international students worldwide, but it does not provide visa sponsorship
Not sure which option is right for you? Our admissions team can help you think through your next steps.

## Information for International Applicants
Application timing depends on your visa status.
If you require a new student visa (F-1): You must apply by the international application deadline. For the Autumn 2026 entry, that deadline has passed. Applicants who require a new visa should plan to apply for a future term.
If you are currently studying in the U.S. and transferring your SEVIS record: You may still be eligible to apply by the final application deadline.

## Application Requirements

### English language proficiency
Applicants to the Master’s in Applied Data Science program who do not meet the English Language Proficiency criteria must submit proof of English language proficiency.
Minimum scores for the Master’s in Applied Data Science program: TOEFL: 102 (prior requirement) or 5 (current requirement; no subscore requirement); IELTS, 7 (no subscore requirement).
